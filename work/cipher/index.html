Cipher

Trading strategies, tested before they are trusted

Overview

An algorithmic trading system for crypto and equities, with a backtesting engine I wrote to compare strategies against historical data before risking anything. Five strategies implemented — grid, scalping, and machine-learning based — each with its own risk manager. The real lesson of this project was not finance: it was that a result you cannot reproduce is not a result.
