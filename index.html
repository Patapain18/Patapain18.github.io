02

What I do

Real-time 3D on the web

Three.js and WebGL scenes that run at 60fps in a browser — and in VR headsets. Custom Blender-to-web pipelines, Draco compression, shader work.

Interface design

I design what I build. Typography, motion, and interaction are not a layer on top of the code — they are the reason the code exists.

Sound & generative audio

Web Audio API, procedural music, MIDI. Every project I ship has a sound identity, because silence is a design decision too.

03

About

I started with a vocational degree in digital systems — soldering boards, fixing machines, configuring networks. I liked understanding how things worked, but I wanted to make things people could feel.

So I moved to multimedia design, and found the exact overlap I was looking for: projects where the engineering and the aesthetics are the same decision. Today I run a small creative studio with a friend, and I ship something almost every week.
